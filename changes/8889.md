Sub-agents: a child's own `TimeoutError` under a delegation budget is no longer reported to the parent as a budget timeout

When a sub-agent raised its own `TimeoutError` (for example an internal tool operation timing out) while `SubAgent.timeout_seconds` was set, the parent was told the sub-agent had "exceeded its time budget" and the original exception was lost. The child's failure now follows the crash contract: it propagates by default, is contained as a child crash under `contain_errors=True`, and is replaced by `on_failure` when set. Genuine delegation budget expiries are unchanged.
